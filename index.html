Few words about me

I can't walk past a product without filming it.

My name is Aneta and I have one problem: I can\'t walk past a product without immediately wanting to film it. I make UGC content that feels like a recommendation from a friend, while still showing the best of what your product has to offer.

Lifestyle, food, travel, sport, beauty, home decor, mall stores, apps, online products, events… basically anything that can be shown to the world.

I\'ve worked with 35+ brands across beauty, food, fashion, events and apps. The list keeps growing, which is the kind of proof I find most reassuring.
